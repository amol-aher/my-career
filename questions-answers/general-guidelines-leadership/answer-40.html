Approach to Learning New Technologies After Many Years

Mindset: Stay curious and pragmatic — evaluate tools for fit and cost.

Steps

  1. Identify the problem the new tech solves.
  2. Run a small PoC to validate assumptions.
  3. Measure against criteria: performance, team ramp, ecosystem maturity.
  4. Gradual adoption via pilot projects and internal training.

Example

When adopting container orchestration, we started with a dev cluster, moved a non-critical service, tracked metrics, and iterated before wider rollout.
