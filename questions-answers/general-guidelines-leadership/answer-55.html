Making Tradeoffs Between Engineering Excellence and Speed

Decision factors

  • Business urgency and potential revenue impact
  • Technical risk and operational cost
  • Reversibility of the change
  • Long-term maintenance burden

Approach

  1. Quantify business value and technical cost (TCO) where possible.
  2. Identify mitigations: feature flags, canary releases, increased monitoring.
  3. Implement minimal viable solution and schedule technical debt remediation.
  4. Monitor closely and iterate.

Example

We had to launch a time-sensitive marketing experiment. We shipped a simpler implementation that duplicated some functionality but used a flag to control traffic. Post-launch, we measured impact and then refactored the duplicated logic into a shared service once ROI was proven.

# Pseudo-plan
- MVP: implement feature as isolated endpoint behind flag (2 weeks)
- Measure: 2 weeks of metrics collection
- Refactor: merge logic into shared service (next sprint)
