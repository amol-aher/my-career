Resolving a Conflict Within the Team — Example

Scenario

Two engineers disagreed over a schema design: one prioritized normalization, the other prioritized query performance for a reporting feature.

Approach

  1. Private 1:1s to understand perspectives.
  2. Joint design session to list requirements and constraints.
  3. Benchmarked both approaches with realistic data and generated metrics.
  4. Chose a hybrid approach: normalized schema with precomputed denormalized read models for reporting.

Outcome

Both engineers contributed to the final design, felt heard, and gained trust. The hybrid solution met performance needs and maintained data integrity.
