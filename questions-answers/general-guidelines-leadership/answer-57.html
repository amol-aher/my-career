Evaluating Build vs Buy Decisions at Scale

Criteria to consider

  • Strategic differentiation — is this core to our product?
  • Time to market — how fast do we need it?
  • Total cost of ownership — initial and ongoing maintenance costs.
  • Integration complexity and vendor lock-in risk.
  • Security, compliance, and data residency requirements.
  • Support & SLAs from a vendor versus internal support costs.

Decision matrix (example)

# Simple scoring example (0-5)
criteria: [strategic_value, time_to_market, tco, integration_risk, security]
build: [5, 2, 3, 2, 4]  # sum = 16
buy:   [2, 5, 4, 3, 3]  # sum = 17 -> buy chosen in this example

Example — Search: Elasticsearch vs Algolia

  • Build (Elasticsearch): more control, better for custom ranking and analytics, operational overhead higher.
  • Buy (Algolia): faster to ship, great developer experience, cost higher at scale and less customizable.

We prototyped both for a sample index, measured relevance, latency, and TCO estimates, then chose based on prioritization of customization vs time-to-market.
