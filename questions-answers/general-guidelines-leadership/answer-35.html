Ensuring Effective Communication with Remote Teams

Key elements: Clear documentation, async-first culture, regular check-ins, and inclusive meetings.

  • Use a single source of truth (confluence/Notion) for decisions.
  • Hold weekly syncs and regular 1:1s.
  • Set norms for response times and meeting etiquette.
  • Leverage async updates (recorded demos, written summaries).

Invest in team rituals that build rapport (virtual coffee, show-and-tell).
