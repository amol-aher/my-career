Philosophy on Delegation

Principles

  • Delegate for growth: Give stretch assignments to grow team members.
  • Match skills to work: Align critical work with experienced engineers; exploratory work can go to junior/mid engineers with support.
  • Retain key ownership: Keep architecture spikes, sensitive incidents, or high-risk integrations under my closer supervision.
  • Trust but verify: Provide guidance upfront, then review outcomes (not micromanage).

How I decide

I decide what I work on vs delegate based on:

  • Criticality to delivery timelines
  • Learning opportunity for the engineer
  • Whether it requires specialized context I hold
  • Team workload distribution
