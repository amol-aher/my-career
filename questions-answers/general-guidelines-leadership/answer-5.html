Resource Allocation for New Feature Requests

High-level Process

  1. Intake & Clarify: Gather acceptance criteria, success metrics, user personas, and constraints.
  2. Estimate & Breakdown: Create an MVP scope, break down into tasks/stories, and produce rough-level estimates (T-shirt sizing and story points).
  3. Prioritize: Use RICE/ICE value vs effort and align with roadmap and stakeholders.
  4. Allocate: Assign a cross-functional deliverable: 1–2 engineers, QA, designer, and product owner depending on scope.
  5. Plan & Execute: Set milestones, CI/CD rollout (feature flags), and monitoring for post-launch.

Detailed Scenario

Suppose the client requests a new analytics dashboard with custom filtering and export. I would:

  • Run a 2–3 day discovery with PM and designer to produce an MVP UI and data contract.
  • Estimate backend work: schema changes, ETL/aggregation, API endpoints. Frontend: Vue components and client-side filtering.
  • Assign a small feature team: 2 backend engineers (one senior, one mid), 1 frontend engineer, 1 QA, 1 designer part-time, and product manager oversight.
  • Plan sprints: MVP in 3 sprints with incremental releases behind a feature flag and telemetry.

Subquestions & Answers

How do you handle urgent interrupts?

Maintain a small buffer of capacity (~10–15%) for urgent bugs or small client requests. For major interrupts, reprioritize and communicate trade-offs to stakeholders.

How to scale when team is busy?

Decompose the feature into independent vertical slices, do parallel work on decoupled parts, or hire contractors/temporary resources for clearly scoped tasks with senior oversight.

  else

  end
end
