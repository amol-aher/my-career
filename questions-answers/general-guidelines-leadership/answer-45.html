Handling Technical Debt in a Large, Long‑Lived Codebase

Principles

  • Make debt visible and measurable.
  • Triage by risk and impact.
  • Schedule regular pay-down work and integrate into sprint planning.

Practices

  • Maintain a technical debt backlog with owners and estimates.
  • Allocate a percentage of each sprint for debt reduction.
  • Use metrics (test coverage, cyclomatic complexity, performance regressions) to prioritize.
  • Create "debt sprints" before major releases when needed.
