How I Mentor Others — My Philosophy

Philosophy

  • Empower, don’t micromanage: Give mentees ownership and responsibility early.
  • Tailor guidance: Different people need different mentoring styles — some prefer hands-on pairing, others prefer frameworks and reading material.
  • Constructive feedback: Feedback is most effective when timely, specific, and actionable.
  • Model behavior: Demonstrate best practices in code, communication, and professionalism.
  • Growth mindset: Encourage experimentation and learning from failure.

How I Mentor

  • Run structured 1:1s with mentees — discuss goals, challenges, and skills they want to improve.
  • Set learning projects — e.g., small services or internal tools to practice architecture and delivery.
  • Encourage public speaking or knowledge-sharing within the team.
  • Provide stretch assignments while ensuring safety nets (reviews, pair programming).

Examples

I mentored a mid-level engineer to lead a payments integration project. We paired on design, I reviewed early PRs closely, and gradually stepped back as he gained confidence. Within 6 months, he independently owned a release end-to-end.
