Mentoring Someone Smarter or Above in Title

Experience

Yes — mentorship isn’t strictly hierarchical. I have mentored senior engineers and peers in areas where I had deeper expertise (Rails internals, AWS, architecture). In return, they often mentored me in their areas of strength.

Philosophy

Mentorship is a two-way relationship. Even if someone has a higher title, there are always skills or domains where I can provide value. Mutual respect and humility are essential.

Example

I coached a senior engineer (technically above me in title) in improving observability practices. He was excellent in algorithms but had less exposure to production monitoring. I introduced tools like Prometheus/Grafana, and we co-created SLO dashboards together. In return, I learned new algorithmic techniques from him.
