The Role of Senior Engineers in Setting Technical Direction

Core responsibilities

  • Propose and evangelize technical vision aligned with business objectives.
  • Establish standards, best practices, and architecture guardrails.
  • Mentor and develop engineering talent; raise the technical bar.
  • Drive pragmatic decision-making and own critical technical projects.
  • Be a bridge between engineering and product/leadership.

How to influence without formal authority

  • Build credibility by shipping results and writing ADRs.
  • Engage stakeholders early and present data-backed tradeoffs.
  • Run prototypes and demos to show value.
  • Form coalitions (guilds) to align cross-team efforts.

Deliverables that matter

  • Roadmaps, ADRs, reference implementations, and migration plans.
  • Metrics that show impact (reduced latency, lower costs, improved deployment frequency).
