Why I Left Adroit and Joined Jombay

Short answer

I left Adroit to join Jombay because the new role offered broader product responsibility, a clearer path to leadership, and the chance to work on a domain with bigger customer impact. There were also opportunities at Jombay to modernize the platform and influence engineering practices at scale.

Detailed reasons

  • Broader scope: Jombay wanted someone to own entire product verticals, not just components; that matched my desire to own end‑to‑end outcomes.
  • Technical challenge: Jombay’s roadmap included scaling challenges (multi-tenant billing, high-throughput ingestion) that aligned with my interests.
  • Career growth: Explicit leadership responsibilities and a path to manage larger teams.
  • Cultural fit: Their product-first mindset and willingness to invest in engineering maturity appealed to me.

How I framed this in interviews

I focused on positive career motives rather than negative reasons for leaving — emphasis on opportunity, impact, and alignment with long-term goals.
