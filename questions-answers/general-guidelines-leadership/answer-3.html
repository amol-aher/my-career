Leading a Team — Confirmation of Role

Short Answer

Yes — I am leading (or have led) engineering teams. My responsibilities include a mixture of technical leadership and people management depending on the organization and context.

What "leading" entails

  • Coaching engineers and driving career growth
  • Owning delivery timelines, risks, and communication with stakeholders
  • Ensuring quality (code reviews, testing, observability)
  • Hiring and building team culture
