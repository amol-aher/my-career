Example Where Communication Was a Challenge

Scenario

During a cross‑functional launch, engineering built a minor but impactful change to an API response format. Product and customer success were not looped in, and some clients relied on the old response shape. The result: customer confusion and a couple of support tickets.

Root causes

  • Assumption that the change was 'minor' and wouldn’t affect clients.
  • Lack of a change-notice process for API contract modifications.
  • Insufficient end-to-end stakeholder communication for client-facing changes.

Remediation

  1. Rolled back the change quickly and prioritized a compatibility mode.
  2. Introduced an API-change process: documentation, deprecation windows, and email/webhook notices to affected clients.
  3. Added a checklist to the PR template: "Does this change client-facing contracts?" If yes, involve Product and CS before merge.

What I learned

Even small technical changes can have large business impacts. Always assume external dependencies and codify communication steps for contract changes.
