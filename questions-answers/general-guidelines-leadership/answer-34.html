Approach to Mentoring Junior Developers

Principles: Provide psychological safety, structured growth paths, and hands-on learning.

Practical tactics

  • Pair programming and guided code reviews.
  • Small, meaningful tickets with clear acceptance criteria.
  • Learning plans with milestones: testing, debugging, architecture basics.
  • Encourage knowledge-sharing and reading groups.

Measurement

Track progress via ramp time, PR autonomy, and confidence in owning features.
