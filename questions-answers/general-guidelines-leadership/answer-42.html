Balancing Clean, Maintainable Code with Tight Deadlines

Principles

  • Prioritize correctness and safety: shipping fast is important but not at the cost of catastrophic bugs.
  • Incremental quality: apply small, meaningful improvements (refactors) as part of feature work.
  • Risk-based approach: spend more time on critical paths and simpler, more pragmatic approaches elsewhere.

Practical tactics

  • Keep PRs small and focused — easier to review and less risky.
  • Use feature flags to ship incomplete or risky work safely.
  • Add tests for the most critical behavior; leave non-critical tests for later.
  • Defer abstraction until there is clear duplication or complexity (YAGNI).
  • Allocate a small % of every sprint (e.g., 10–20%) for maintenance/refactoring.

Example

When shipping a new checkout flow under tight deadline, we implemented the MVP with clear boundaries and added a follow-up ticket to refactor shared logic and add comprehensive tests in the next sprint.
