Leadership Experience — Managing Small Teams

Roles Held

  • Tech lead for a 5-person squad (full-stack)
  • Engineering manager for an 8-person cross-functional team (backend, frontend, QA)
  • Interim Head of Engineering overseeing multiple small teams (total ~18)

Responsibilities

  • Delivery: Set sprint goals, remove blockers, and coordinate launches.
  • Hiring: Sourced candidates, technical interviews, and cultural fit assessments.
  • Career development: Regular 1:1s, promotion mentoring, and growth plans.
  • Operational excellence: Set up SLOs, runbooks, and on-call policies.

Example — Building a Cross-functional Team

I hired two backend engineers and one frontend to form a team to ship a payments integration. I led the architecture, divided work into vertical slices, and instituted a weekly demo to sync with product and design.

Subquestions & Answers

How do you run 1:1s?

Open agenda, career progression topics, recent wins & blockers, feedback in both directions, and clear action items recorded in the next meeting's notes.

How do you motivate a small team?

Set achievable short-term goals, public recognition, autonomy in technical choices, and a path for ownership and career growth.
