Time for Hobbies with Work and Family

Short answer: Yes — I prioritize hobbies alongside work and family through time management and clear boundaries.

How I balance

  • Schedule non-negotiable personal time (evenings or weekends) for family and hobbies.
  • Batch work tasks and use focused blocks to maximize productivity.
  • Communicate availability with the team and negotiate flexible schedules when necessary.
  • Use PTO deliberately to recharge and pursue longer hobbies like travel or photography trips.

Example

I reserve two weekend mornings per month for longer photography outings and keep evenings for family. This helps maintain creativity without impacting delivery.
