Measuring Team Success Beyond Delivering Features

Key dimensions

  • Operational health: MTTR, MTTD, incident frequency, and severity.
  • Delivery quality: defect rates post-release, rollback frequency, automated test coverage.
  • Product impact: adoption, retention, and NPS for features the team owns.
  • Developer experience: cycle time, deployment frequency, developer satisfaction (eNPS), ramp time for new hires.
  • Team growth: promotions, internal mobility, mentoring outcomes.

How I track these

  • Dashboards in Datadog/CloudWatch/Prometheus for reliability metrics.
  • CI metrics (build times, flakiness) and Git metrics (PR sizes, review times).
  • Quarterly engagement surveys and regular 1:1s.
  • Customer feedback & support ticket volume tied to owned areas.

Subquestions & Answers

How do you avoid vanity metrics?

Focus on metrics that map to outcomes: lower MTTR, higher user retention, faster lead time for changes. Avoid raw counts without context.

How do you act on low scores?

Identify root causes (through retros, incident reviews), set improvement experiments, and measure progress with specific targets.
