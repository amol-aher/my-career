Hobby Outside of Work

My Hobby

One of my hobbies is photography, especially street and landscape photography.

Why it’s cool

  • It balances analytical engineering work with a creative outlet.
  • Improves observation skills — noticing patterns, symmetry, and details in everyday life.
  • Gives me perspective and patience; waiting for the right light teaches timing.
  • Provides a way to tell stories visually — similar to storytelling in software design.
