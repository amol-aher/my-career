Instance of a Technical Decision with Long‑Term Impact

Decision

Introduced feature-flag driven trunk-based development and automated canary deployments.

Why it mattered

  • Reduced release risk and made rollbacks simpler.
  • Increased deployment frequency and confidence.
  • Encouraged smaller changes and better review practices.

Positive long-term effects

  • Higher velocity with fewer regressions.
  • Improved developer experience and less fear of shipping.
  • Better observability and metrics tied to feature flags.

Negative example

Conversely, an early decision to centrally manage a shared DB schema across many teams created coupling and slowed independent deployments — we later mitigated this by introducing service-specific read models and API gateways.
