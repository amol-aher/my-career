Why Change Now? — Professional Motivation

Short Answer

Seeking new challenges: larger product scope, opportunity to architect systems at scale, grow a team, and work on modern tooling stacks. Also motivated to join an environment where I can have broader impact on product and engineering culture.

Detailed Reasons

  • Scale: Looking to move from squad-level to organization-level influence, solving systemic engineering problems.
  • Technical growth: Desire to work with newer infra (Kubernetes, observability at scale, event-driven architecture) and modern frontend (complex SPAs or hybrid approaches).
  • Leadership growth: Hire and mentor senior engineers and build reliable delivery practices.
  • Alignment: Seeking a company whose product mission aligns with my values (user-centric design, data-informed decisions).

Subquestions & Answers

Are you leaving because of problems?

Not primarily. While every role has challenges, my decision is proactive — to find opportunities that match my next-stage career goals.

How soon could you join?

I prefer to negotiate notice periods transparently; typically 2–4 weeks for consulting roles and 4–8 weeks for full-time positions depending on transition responsibilities.
