Most Technically Complex System

System: A real-time analytics ingestion and aggregation platform for multi-tenant customers.

Complexities

  • High throughput (millions of events/day) while ensuring per-tenant isolation.
  • Exactly-once processing challenges with idempotency and deduplication.
  • Balancing batch vs stream processing for cost and latency.
  • Operational concerns: partitioning, scaling workers, backpressure handling, and monitoring.

Approach

Used modular pipeline components, idempotent consumers, time-windowed aggregations, and robust observability with alerts and dashboards.
