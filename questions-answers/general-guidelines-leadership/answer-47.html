Mentoring and Coaching Junior/Mid Engineers — Success Story

Approach

  • Pair programming, guided PRs, and clear feedback.
  • Personalized learning plans and weekly checkpoints.
  • Stretch assignments and gradual autonomy.

Success story

A mid-level engineer struggled with system design and ownership. Over 9 months: pairing sessions, design practice assignments, and incremental ownership of a service led to promotion to senior engineer. He later led a critical migration project successfully.
