Do I Like Leading Teams?

Answer

Yes — I genuinely enjoy leading teams. Leadership allows me to multiply impact: instead of only my code shipping, I enable the whole team to deliver high-quality products. Mentoring, setting direction, and unblocking others is rewarding.

Why

  • Creating an environment where engineers can thrive gives me satisfaction.
  • Building culture and processes that scale is a challenging and fulfilling problem.
  • Helping people grow in their careers is one of the most rewarding aspects.
