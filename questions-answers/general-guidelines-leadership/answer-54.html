Balancing Hands-on Coding with Architecture and Leadership

Framework

I balance via context-based allocation: when the team needs a technical anchor (new architecture, spike) I invest more hands-on time; when the team needs scaling, hiring, or stakeholder management I shift to leadership.

Weekly example split

Mon: 30% code review, 30% meetings, 40% design/prototyping
Tue: 60% coding (deep work), 20% 1:1s, 20% docs
Wed: 50% meetings (stakeholders), 25% mentoring, 25% code review
Thu: 40% architecture & planning, 30% coding, 30% hiring/interviews
Fri: 60% heads-down coding or unblock, 20% team demo, 20% learning

How I stay relevant technically

  • Do periodic deep-coding sprints.
  • Review core PRs and keep tabs on system health.
  • Run office hours or pairing sessions for technical topics.
