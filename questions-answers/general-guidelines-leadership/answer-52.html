Leading Through Ambiguity or Incomplete Requirements

Strategy

  • Rapid discovery: identify assumptions, unknowns, and risks.
  • Make small, testable bets: build prototypes or MVPs to validate assumptions.
  • Prioritize outcomes over features: define success metrics.
  • Iterate with short feedback loops and frequent stakeholder check-ins.

Example

We had to build an integration with a partner whose API was unstable and under-documented. I ran a 2-week discovery that included:

  • Ask the partner for sandbox access and sample payloads.
  • Build a small adapter prototype to exercise edge cases.
  • Define an MVP that served 10 customers with clear fallbacks.
  • Document contract expectations and error-handling behavior.

Artifacts I produce

  • Assumption map
  • Risk register
  • MVP scope and acceptance criteria
  • Prototype code and metrics for validation
# Sample simple assumption map (YAML)
assumptions:
  - partner_api_supports_pagination: unknown
  - partner_retry_semantics: unknown
risks:
  - large_payloads_might_timeout: high
mitigations:
  - use streaming & pagination; set timeouts & retries

Subquestions & Answers

How do you keep the team motivated?

Provide clarity on short-term goals, celebrate small wins (spikes completed), and rotate ownership so everyone feels contribution and progress.

How do you get stakeholder buy-in?

Share prototype demos, explicit risks, expected outcomes, and success metrics. Ask stakeholders to commit to decision gates for further investment.
