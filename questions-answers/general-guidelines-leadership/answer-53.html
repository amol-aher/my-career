Ensuring Knowledge Sharing Across a Large Engineering Team

Core practices

  • Centralized documentation: ADRs, runbooks, onboarding guides in Notion/Confluence/Git repos.
  • Regular tech talks and brown-bags.
  • Guilds/chapters by domain (e.g., frontend guild, infra guild).
  • Rotation programs: temporary cross-team rotations for learning.
  • Pairing and code reviews with mentorship focus.

Artifacts and structure

  • Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) for major decisions.
  • Runbooks accessible from the incident channel.
  • Tagged documentation and a discovery onboarding checklist.
# Example ADR title: Use Kafka for ingestion pipeline
Status: Accepted
Context: Need high-throughput, durable messaging with consumer groups
Decision: Adopt Kafka with partitioning per tenant
Consequences: Operational overhead; easier horizontal scaling

Measuring effectiveness

  • Onboarding time for new hires (ramp time metric).
  • Number of incidents attributable to knowledge gaps.
  • Participation in tech talks and internal surveys.
