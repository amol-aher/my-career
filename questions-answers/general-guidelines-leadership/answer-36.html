Communication Without Daily Standups

Strategies: Use async updates, clear owners, and periodic demos.

  • Daily async status in a shared channel or a short ticket comment.
  • Use well-defined tickets with acceptance criteria and blockers fields.
  • Weekly or bi-weekly demos and retro for alignment.
  • Adopt dashboards for progress (CI status, deploys, issue counts).

Ensure any blocker is flagged immediately via priority channels.
