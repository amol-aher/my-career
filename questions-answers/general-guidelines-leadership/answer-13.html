Work‑Life Balance at Jombay

Overview

Work‑life balance varied over time and by project. During product launches or critical incidents, the cadence naturally intensified; otherwise, there was an emphasis on sustainable pacing and respect for personal time.

Practical examples

  • Normal cadence: Standard working hours, asynchronous communication where possible, flexible start times, and policies to discourage after-hours messaging unless urgent.
  • High-pressure phases: Pre-launch and incident windows could require extended hours for short periods — we tried to compensate with time‑off or lighter loads afterward.
  • Manager support: Managers were encouraged to balance team loads and watch for burnout signals; regular 1:1s were used to check wellbeing.

How I helped improve balance

  • Advocated for rotating on-call schedules and backfill so individuals weren't repeatedly pulled into nights.
  • Encouraged clear "end of day" expectations for non-urgent communications and enforced calendar-free afternoons for heads-down work when possible.

Outcome

Overall, balance was reasonable with expected peaks. The culture valued personal time but accepted occasional, short-lived sprints when business needs demanded it.
