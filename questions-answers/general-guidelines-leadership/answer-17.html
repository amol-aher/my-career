What My Supervisor Would Say About My Improvement Areas

Likely feedback

  • Delegation: At times I take on more hands‑on work than necessary; could delegate more to grow the team.
  • Over‑engineering: Occasionally I introduce patterns or abstractions earlier than needed.
  • Work-life boundaries: Willingness to help can sometimes lead to taking on extra hours; could push back earlier.

How I’m addressing these

  • Practicing clearer delegation with ownership docs and tech-mentors for features.
  • Using simpler YAGNI-first designs and only abstracting when there’s clear repetition or variance.
  • Setting explicit “no meeting” blocks and protecting the team’s focus time.
