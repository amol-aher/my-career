Tools & Practices for Scalability, Reliability, and Performance

Observability & Monitoring

  • Metrics: Prometheus, CloudWatch, Datadog
  • Tracing: Jaeger, OpenTelemetry
  • Logging: ELK/EFK, structured logs

Testing & CI/CD

  • Automated test suites, performance regression tests
  • CI pipelines with parallelization and caching
  • Canary and blue/green deployments

Architectural Practices

  • Auto-scaling, load balancing, and stateless services
  • Event-driven design for decoupling and scalability
  • Caching strategies (Redis, CDN)
  • Database sharding, read replicas, and careful indexing

Operational Practices

  • SLOs/SLIs and error budgets
  • Runbooks, incident playbooks, and postmortems
  • Chaos engineering for resilience testing
