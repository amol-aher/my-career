Handling Technical Disagreements and Conflicts

Approach: Encourage data-driven, respectful conversations and focus on tradeoffs rather than personal positions.

Steps

  1. Listen and surface concerns in 1:1s.
  2. Collect data (benchmarks, prototypes, cost estimates).
  3. Host a design review with clear criteria and acceptance factors.
  4. Seek compromise via experiments or abstraction layers.
  5. If needed, escalate to a neutral senior or use decision-by-consensus/timeboxed voting.

Outcome focus

Align on measurable outcomes and revisit decisions after iteration.
