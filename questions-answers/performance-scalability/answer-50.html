Implementing Circuit Breakers in Microservices

What a circuit breaker does

Prevents repeated calls to a failing service by opening the circuit after thresholds are hit, allowing it to recover.

Libraries & approaches

  • Java: Resilience4j
  • Ruby: circuitbox (uses Redis)
  • Service mesh: Istio provides circuit breaking policies at the sidecar level

Simple Circuitbox example (Ruby)

require 'circuitbox'

cb = Circuitbox.circuit(:users_service,
  exceptions: [Timeout::Error, Errno::ECONNREFUSED],
  sleep_window: 30, # seconds to try again
  volume_threshold: 5,
  error_threshold: 50 # percent
)

cb.run do
  RestClient.get('https://users.example/api/v1/profile')
end

Best practices

  • Combine timeouts + retries + circuit breakers
  • Expose circuit state metrics and alerts
  • Use bulkheads and throttles alongside circuit breakers
