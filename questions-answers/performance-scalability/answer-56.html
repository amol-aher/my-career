EC2, ECS, EKS, Lambda — AWS Compute Options & When to Use Them

EC2 (virtual machines)

Full control over OS — choose when you need custom runtime, GPUs, or legacy apps. Higher operational overhead (patching, scaling).

ECS (Elastic Container Service)

Managed container orchestration. Use Fargate for serverless containers to avoid managing EC2. Good for containerized workloads with easier ops.

EKS (Elastic Kubernetes Service)

Managed Kubernetes for teams with Kubernetes expertise, need for complex scheduling, custom controllers, and portability.

Lambda (serverless)

Function-as-a-Service for event-driven workloads. Great for infrequent or highly variable workloads; cost-effective but with cold starts and execution time limits.

Choosing guidance

  • Choose Lambda for short-lived event-driven tasks and microservices with low operational overhead.
  • Choose ECS/Fargate for straightforward container-based apps without running a Kubernetes control plane.
  • Choose EKS if you need Kubernetes ecosystem features or multi-cluster control.
  • Choose EC2 for full control, legacy lift-and-shift, or very specialized needs.
