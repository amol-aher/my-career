AWS Auto Scaling — How It Works & Handling Spikes

How Auto Scaling works

Auto Scaling Groups (ASG) add/remove instances based on policies: target tracking, step scaling, scheduled scaling. For containers, ECS/EKS autoscaling works at service/task level.

Strategies for unpredictable spikes

  • Target tracking on request/target metrics: Use ALB request count per target or custom metrics (queue depth) rather than CPU.
  • Warm pool / pre-warming: Keep a small number of standby instances warm for rapid scale-out.
  • Scale-out grace and cooldown: Tune cooldown and scale-in protection to avoid oscillation.
  • Use SQS/ECS autoscaling based on queue length: Buffer work in queues and scale consumers by depth.
  • Predictive scaling: AWS Predictive Scaling can anticipate patterns.

Practical tips

  • Prefer horizontal scaling with ASG and autoscaling policies based on business metrics.
  • Test scaling behavior with load tests to ensure safe thresholds and warm-up times.
  • Consider spot instances for worker clusters to reduce cost with fallbacks.
