Key Metrics to Monitor for Microservices

Three golden signals

  • Latency: p50, p95, p99 for requests and RPCs.
  • Traffic / Throughput: requests per second, messages per second.
  • Errors: error rates, 5xx, retries, failed jobs.

Other important metrics

  • Resource saturation: CPU, memory, disk IO.
  • DB metrics: connection count, slow queries, locks.
  • Queue metrics: queue depth, consumer lag, lag growth rate.
  • Circuit breaker states and counts.
  • Service-level SLOs and budget burn rate.

Logging & traces

Correlation IDs, traces (sampled), and structured logs for root-cause analysis.
