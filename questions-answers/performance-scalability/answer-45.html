Designing Service Communication to Avoid Bottlenecks

Principles

  • Asynchronous by default: Prefer event-driven patterns for non-critical interactions.
  • Backpressure & queueing: Use queues (Kafka, SQS) to buffer bursts and autoscale consumers.
  • Partitioning: Partition messages by key to allow parallel processing without contention.
  • Cache frequently-read data: Local caches or read models prevent hitting other services frequently.
  • Graceful degradation: Serve degraded responses when downstreams are slow.

Techniques

  • Use bulk/batched APIs instead of one-by-one calls.
  • Use rate limits and quotas to protect critical services.
  • Introduce a service mesh (Istio) for fine-grained traffic control and retries.
  • Use CDN for static and public content.
