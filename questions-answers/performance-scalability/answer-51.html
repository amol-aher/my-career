Detecting and Handling Cascading Failures

Detection

  • Monitor error rates, latency p95/p99, and active connections.
  • Watch queue growth and consumer lag.
  • Use distributed tracing to find spike origins.

Mitigation

  • Circuit breakers to stop repeated failing calls.
  • Bulkheads to isolate resource pools.
  • Rate limiting and backpressure to reduce incoming load.
  • Graceful degradation: serve cached or reduced functionality.

Example incident flow

  1. Service A latency spikes -> A times out calling B -> A's retries increase load on B -> B fails -> cascade.
  2. Fix: open circuit on A for B, apply rate-limits, scale B if appropriate, remove retries or add jitter.
