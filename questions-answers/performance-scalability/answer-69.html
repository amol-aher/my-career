Designing Dashboards for Microservice Health on AWS

Guiding principles

  • Focus on the three golden signals: latency, traffic, errors, plus saturation.
  • Show SLO/SLA compliance and burn rates.
  • Provide drill-down capability from service-level to infrastructure-level metrics.
  • Make dashboards actionable: include runbook links and recent deploys.

Key panels for a service dashboard

  • Request p50/p95/p99 latency over time
  • Request rate (RPS) and error rate (4xx/5xx)
  • CPU, memory, disk I/O for pods/instances
  • DB connections, replica lag
  • Queue depth and consumer lag (if applicable)
  • Circuit breaker and retry counts
  • Recent deploys and health check status

Tools

CloudWatch Dashboards, Grafana (CloudWatch datasource), and distributed tracing views (Jaeger/OTel) are commonly combined.
