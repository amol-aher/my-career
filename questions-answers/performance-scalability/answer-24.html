When Stakeholders Say "Skip It" — How to Respond

Stakeholders sometimes deprioritize technical fixes. The goal is to justify the work quantitatively, escalate when necessary, and accept pragmatic trade-offs.

Steps I follow

  1. Quantify risk/cost: Show potential impact (technical debt, outages, user impact, future cost) in numbers if possible.
  2. Offer alternatives: e.g., partial fixes, monitoring-only, or a timeboxed spike.
  3. Document decision: Capture the decision and the acceptance of risk in the ADR or ticket.
  4. Revisit periodically: Put it on a tech-debt backlog with SLA for reassessment.

When to escalate

If the issue is a safety, compliance, or major revenue risk, escalate to product leadership with evidence and remediation options.

Example

Problem: Single point of failure in payment worker.
Cost: 4 hours of downtime estimated per incident; average $X revenue lost.
Option A: Full rewrite (3 sprints).
Option B: Add monitoring + circuit breaker + failover (2 weeks).
Stakeholder decision: Accept B now, schedule A later.
