How I've combined these services

Common patterns I've built include: backend services in EKS talking to RDS (multi-AZ) with ElastiCache for session/caching, frontends in S3+CloudFront, background jobs in Fargate tasks, and observability via CloudWatch + Grafana where needed.

Subquestions interviewers often ask

  • Which services reduce operational burden? Managed services like RDS/Aurora, EKS (managed control plane), Lambda and Fargate abstract away infrastructure operations.
  • When to prefer serverless? For spiky or event-driven workloads with unpredictable scale, or when you want to minimize server ops and pay-per-use.
  • Costs? Monitor CloudWatch billing metrics and use Cost Explorer; mix spot instances, savings plans and reserved instances to control compute costs.
