Interview subquestions (examples)

How do you pick services during architecture?

Start with non-functional requirements: availability, latency, scaling model, operational overhead and cost. Prefer managed services where operational burden is high and cost is acceptable; choose self-managed (EKS/EC2) where you need fine-grained control.

How do you handle multi-cloud concerns?

Design abstraction layers where possible (service contracts, portable IaC) and keep cloud-specific services limited to the edges (e.g., direct use of BigQuery for analytics vs common APIs for app logic). Emphasize testing and deployment automation in each target cloud.

What about compliance?

Implement encryption at rest/in transit, audit trails, least-privilege access, and automate security (CIS benchmarks, automated scans) using CSPM tools and IaC scanning. Maintain documented SOPs and run regular penetration tests.
