System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: Yes — typically the PO defines the business epics and user stories; the engineering team collaboratively creates the sub-tasks and defines technical acceptance criteria.

Grooming ceremony responsibilities
  • PO: clarifies business intent, priority, and success metrics.
  • Engineering: identifies technical tasks, risks, and estimates.
  • QA: defines test scenarios and regression impact.
Why this separation works

The PO focuses on outcomes while engineering focuses on delivery. This ensures shared ownership and prevents requirements from being too implementation-focused or too vague.
