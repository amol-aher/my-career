Establishing credibility when joining a new company

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: Building credibility is a mix of listening, delivering predictable value, transparency, and relationship building. This is done through early wins, clear communication, and visible ownership.

Actions I take (first 90 days)
  1. Listen & learn: meet stakeholders, run discovery calls with product, ops, and customers to surface pain points.
  2. Document findings: create a 30/60/90 plan outlining priorities and hypotheses for improvements.
  3. Deliver small, visible wins: pick a few high-impact, low-risk improvements (e.g., fix a recurring production issue, improve a slow page) and deliver quickly.
  4. Be transparent: communicate what I know, what I don’t, and the plan to close gaps. Use demos and short summaries.
  5. Build relationships: offer pairing sessions, attend customer calls, and volunteer for on-call rotations to show commitment.
Concrete examples of credibility-building artifacts
  • Runbook improvements that reduce MTTR by 30%.
  • Performance patch that reduces page load from 2.2s to 850ms with a small scope change.
  • Architecture Decision Record (ADR) that crystallizes a chosen path and mitigations.
How I measure progress
  • Stakeholder sentiment (qualitative) — periodic check-ins.
  • Operational metrics — incident counts and MTTR.
  • Delivery consistency — hit rate of commitments.
