Balancing approachability and authority

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: A leader must be approachable for feedback and collaboration, but authoritative in setting vision and enforcing accountability.

How I balance
  • Approachability: open-door policy, office hours, regular 1:1s, active Slack presence.
  • Authority: set clear expectations, enforce quality standards, and follow through on commitments.
  • Consistency: fair decision-making and transparency build respect.
Example

I encouraged engineers to propose architectural changes, but when two competing designs emerged, I facilitated discussion, then made a final decision aligned with business goals.
