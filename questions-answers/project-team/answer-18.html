Approach to architectural and technical decisions

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: I favor inclusive decision-making: define the problem, propose options, run lightweight design reviews, gather feedback from senior engineers and the team, and make a documented decision (ADR). For critical or emergency decisions I might act quicker but still document rationale.

Decision process
  1. Define goals & constraints: performance, cost, compatibility, timeline.
  2. Research & spikes: prototypes to validate assumptions.
  3. Options & trade-offs: list alternatives with pros/cons.
  4. Design review: invite senior engineers for feedback and consider broader team inputs.
  5. Decision: pick an approach, write an Architecture Decision Record (ADR), and outline migration steps.
When to involve whom
  • Senior engineers: for major platform or cross-service changes.
  • Whole team: for choices affecting day-to-day implementation or team productivity.
  • Stakeholders: for decisions with business impact or cost changes.
Example ADR structure
# ADR: Switch from monolith DB to per-service DBs
Status: Accepted
Context: increasing contention on the shared DB impacting latency
Decision: Move to per-service Postgres instances with async replication for shared reads
Consequences:
- + better isolation & scaling
- - increased operational complexity & backup costs
Implementation plan:
- Spike & benchmark
- Create cross-team migration guide
- Migrate in 3 phases with feature flags
