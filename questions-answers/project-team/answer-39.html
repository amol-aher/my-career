Steps when a project starts slipping behind schedule

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: Reassess priorities, identify blockers, and take corrective actions — re-scope, add resources, or negotiate timeline changes. Keep stakeholders closely informed.

Immediate checklist
  1. Identify the critical path and impacted milestones.
  2. Remove non-essential work and focus on minimal shippable product.
  3. Reallocate or add senior resources to unblock critical tasks.
  4. Increase cadence of checkpoints (daily standups for the squad).
  5. Communicate a revised plan and expected dates.
Example: accelerated recovery

We once moved to a "sprint zero" style where all hands focused on unblockers for two days, paired on risky tasks, and restored a steady velocity within a week.
