Estimation techniques: Planning Poker & Fibonacci

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: We use Planning Poker with Fibonacci sequence (1,2,3,5,8,13...) because it accelerates convergence and encodes uncertainty. For very small or high-level work we use T-shirt sizing or buckets.

Why Fibonacci?
  • Non-linear progression reflects uncertainty growth.
  • Encourages discussion where estimates diverge.
  • Quick to reach consensus for relative sizing.
Estimation meeting best practices
  • Ensure stories are INVEST-compliant (Independent, Negotiable, Valuable, Estimable, Small, Testable).
  • Explain assumptions before voting.
  • Discuss outliers and re-vote after clarifications.
  • Record final chosen estimate and assumptions in ticket comments.
Alternative methods
  • T-shirt sizing for roadmap-level planning.
  • Story slicing: break bigger stories until they fit into a story point size.
