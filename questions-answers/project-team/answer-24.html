Preferred ways to receive feedback on performance and communication

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: I prefer timely, specific feedback complemented by a written action plan. Regular 1:1s are the primary channel, supported by anonymized 360° feedback in formal reviews.

Practical preferences
  • Frequency: weekly or bi-weekly 1:1s for operational feedback; quarterly reviews for long-term goals.
  • Format: direct and private for developmental critique; public recognition for strengths.
  • Content: include examples, impact, and suggested actions.
  • Follow-up: I like a short written note with agreed actions and a timeline.
How I incorporate feedback
  1. Agree on 2–3 concrete actions.
  2. Track progress in my personal development tracker.
  3. Revisit in subsequent 1:1s and request mid-point check-ins if necessary.
