Metrics that indicate scoping or misrequirements

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: Scoping or requirement issues surface as high re-open rates, frequent acceptance criteria changes, many comments/clarification threads, and large variance between estimates and actuals.

Specific indicators
  • Re-open rate: stories moved back from QA/Done to In Progress frequently.
  • Estimate variance: average actual effort is much larger than estimate across multiple stories.
  • Clarification volume: many comments or sub-tasks added post-commit.
  • Regression defects: defects tied to misunderstood requirements.
  • Mid-sprint scope creep: acceptance criteria changed or scope added mid-sprint.
Actions when metrics trigger
  1. Run a quick story-level retro: capture mismatches between assumptions and reality.
  2. Bring PO, QA, and engineering together to rewrite unclear acceptance criteria.
  3. Create a Definition of Ready checklist to avoid future under-specified stories.
  4. Consider a spike to clarify unknowns before committing.
