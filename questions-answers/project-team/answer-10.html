Estimating: time vs story points vs other units

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: I prefer story points for sprint-level planning and team velocity tracking, while using time estimates (ideal days/hours) for short-term capacity planning and stakeholder communication.

Why story points?
  • Captures relative effort, complexity, and uncertainty.
  • Team-specific and normalizes across varying skill levels.
  • Enables velocity-based forecasting.
When to use time estimates
  • Stakeholder asks for a calendar date or when onboarding a new member to gauge immediate load.
  • Tasks that are time-boxed or require contractor billing.
Conversion & practical tips
  • Do not convert points to hours rigidly; use historical velocity to forecast.
  • For new teams, use conservative velocity and re-calibrate after 3–4 sprints.
  • Keep a mix: story points for planning, hours for day-to-day task tracking when necessary.

# Team velocity = 30 story points per sprint (2 weeks)
# Backlog = 90 story points
# Required sprints = 90 / 30 = 3 sprints (6 weeks)
