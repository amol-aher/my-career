Handling a struggling or underperforming team member

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: Diagnose root cause, create a supportive improvement plan, provide coaching and measurable objectives, and involve HR if needed. Focus on empathy and clarity.

Diagnosis steps
  1. One-on-one to understand the situation (skill gaps, personal issues, unclear expectations).
  2. Review recent work together: PRs, tickets, tests, and behavior patterns.
  3. Identify whether the core issue is skills, motivation, or environment.
Improvement plan template
Improvement Plan - Name: Jane Doe
Duration: 60 days
Areas to improve:
1) Code quality & tests - Goal: reduce PR rework by 50%
2) Delivery predictability - Goal: finish assigned small tasks within sprint
Support:
- Weekly pairing sessions (2h)
- Training: Rails workshop (external course)
- Mentor: assign @senior to shadow and review
Checkpoints:
- 2-week check-in, 4-week review, final 60-day review
When to escalate to HR
  • No measurable improvement despite coaching and support.
  • Behavioral issues that impact team safety.
  • Performance concerns needing formal process.
Example success story

A team member with initial low confidence improved after weekly pairing, clear small tasks, and mentorship, and later became the owner of a critical subsystem.
