Creating sub-tasks: solo vs collaborative

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: Sub-tasks should be collaborative. I often draft an initial decomposition, but final sizing and task ownership are decided with the team during grooming to ensure shared understanding and buy-in.

Why collaborate
  • Captures knowledge from implementers.
  • Uncovers hidden work (infra, monitoring, migrations).
  • Improves estimates and ownership.
My role
  • Provide structure and example subtasks.
  • Highlight cross-cutting concerns (security, performance, data).
  • Ensure tasks meet the Definition of Ready before sprint commitment.
Practical approach during grooming
  1. PO explains intent and acceptance criteria.
  2. I present the draft decomposition.
  3. Team members add/adjust subtasks and provide estimates.
  4. We finalize and assign ownership.
Template for a sub-task entry
Title: Backend: Implement address POST endpoint
Description:
- Controller action + strong params
- Model validations
- Authentication & authorization checks
- Unit & request specs
Estimate: 8h
Owner: @alice
Acceptance: API returns 201 with created address id and persisted data
