Leading projects at Adroit Coders or Jombay — solo vs team-led

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: In service-oriented roles at firms like Adroit Coders or Jombay, projects are a mix of team-led and solo efforts. I led several client projects where I decomposed scope and coordinated teams; smaller prototypes or PoCs were often solo.

Typical engagement patterns
  • Client delivery: cross-functional teams (PM, QA, engineers) with a tech lead responsible for architecture and delivery.
  • Proof-of-concept: rapid solo or small-team work to validate feasibility.
  • Transition to client-run: deliverables often include knowledge transfer, docs, and runbooks.
Example: leading a client project
  1. Kickoff: align on success metrics, constraints, and timelines.
  2. Discovery: technical spikes and integration checks with client systems.
  3. Architecture: propose solution and split into milestones.
  4. Execution: assign tasks, run sprints, coordinate QA and deployment to client environments.
  5. Handover: documentation, training, and support period.
Artifacts I produce
  • 30/60/90 plan
  • Architecture diagrams and ADRs
  • Runbooks and operational checklists
  • Client-facing demo scripts and training materials
