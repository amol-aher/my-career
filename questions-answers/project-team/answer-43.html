Motivating a team during challenging projects

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: Motivation comes from purpose, recognition, autonomy, and visible progress. In tough projects I ensure alignment with mission, remove blockers, and celebrate small wins.

Key practices
  • Clarify purpose: explain the 'why' behind the project and user impact.
  • Set achievable milestones: break down long timelines into visible checkpoints.
  • Recognition: celebrate wins in retros, Slack, and org demos.
  • Psychological safety: encourage open talk about stress or blockers.
  • Support: reallocate resources and shield the team from non-critical tasks.
Example motivational boost

During a long migration project, I organized bi-weekly demos to show progress. Stakeholder praise during demos reinvigorated the team, making the abstract task tangible.
