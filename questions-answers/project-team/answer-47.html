Handling conflicts between team members

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: Conflicts are natural. I resolve them with mediation, focusing on shared goals and respectful communication.

Steps I take
  1. Private conversations with each party to hear perspectives.
  2. Joint meeting with clear rules (respect, listening, focus on issue not person).
  3. Clarify shared goals (project success, team values).
  4. Seek compromise or creative solutions.
  5. Follow up to ensure conflict is resolved.
Example

Two developers disagreed on frontend framework. I facilitated a design review, highlighted shared criteria (performance, learning curve, maintainability), and guided the team to a consensus.
