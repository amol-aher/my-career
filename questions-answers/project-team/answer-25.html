A time I received feedback and integrated it into my behavior

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Scenario: Early in a leadership role I received feedback that my status updates were too detailed for executives and buried the key decision points.

What I changed
  1. Adopted BLUF (Bottom Line Up Front) for executive communications and created one-page summaries highlighting decisions needed.
  2. Used dashboards for data-heavy updates and included a 3-line summary in emails.
  3. Sought peer review for high-visibility messages for the first month to ensure clarity.
Result

Executives reported faster alignment and fewer follow-up questions. This also improved the team's ability to make decisions faster because the context was clearer.

Example before/after
Before:
- 500-word email describing every detail

After:
BLUF: We propose delaying feature X by 2 weeks to fix critical stability issues. Options: A) Delay 2 weeks, B) Ship reduced scope. Recommendation: A.
Metrics: current uptime 99.6% (target 99.9%)
