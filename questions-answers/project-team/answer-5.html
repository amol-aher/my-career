Scrum Masters and Product Owners — role questions

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: Dedicated Scrum Masters and Product Owners are ideal for larger teams; smaller orgs may combine responsibilities with clear boundaries. I've worked in both setups.

Benefits of dedicated roles
  • Scrum Master: focuses on process health, removes impediments, coaches agile practices, and shields the team from distractions.
  • Product Owner: owns backlog prioritization, stakeholder alignment, and acceptance criteria.
When roles are combined
  • Small startups often have the engineering manager or a senior engineer facilitate ceremonies; the founder or PM may act as PO.
  • Key is documenting who makes which decisions (RACI), and ensuring time is allocated to the role functions.
Examples of collaboration
  1. PO writes high-level epics and business goals.
  2. Tech lead breaks epics into technical tasks and acceptance criteria.
  3. SM ensures grooming occurs and helps the team estimate and commit.
