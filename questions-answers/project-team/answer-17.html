Communicating delays or risks to external stakeholders

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: Communicate early, clearly, and with proposed options. Use a short written update and follow-up with a meeting if the impact is material. Tailor the level of detail to the audience.

What to include in the communication
  • Concise summary of the issue and impact.
  • Why it happened (brief root cause).
  • Mitigation steps already taken.
  • Clear recommended options and trade-offs.
  • Revised timeline and owners.
Channels & cadence
  • Email or stakeholder channel for formal record.
  • Optional follow-up meeting for decision-making stakeholders.
  • Weekly status updates until resolved.
Example message
Hi all,

We discovered instability in the 3rd-party identity provider which blocks user migrations. Impact: user migration paused for affected cohorts, potential 3 business day delay.

Actions:
- Running a spike to implement a fallback (in progress)
- Engaged vendor support (ticket #12345)
- Proposal: migrate unaffected cohorts first and delay the remainder by 3 days

Next update: EOD today or sooner if new info.

Regards,
Tech Lead
