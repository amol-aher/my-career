Balancing management tasks with technical contributions

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: I balance by time-boxing, delegating effectively, and keeping a hands-on backlog. The goal is to maximize leverage: enable others while remaining capable of stepping in technically when needed.

Practical strategies
  • Time-box: Block calendars for focused deep work and separate blocks for meetings and 1:1s.
  • Delegate: Empower senior engineers to own subsystems and lead design reviews.
  • Keep a hands-on ticket list: 1–2 tasks per sprint that keep me technically current.
  • Office hours: Scheduled time for pair programming or architecture discussions.
  • Automate & document: Reduce managerial overhead with good docs and automation for routine tasks.
Example weekly split
  • Monday: sprint planning & technical design (40%)
  • Tuesday: coding deep work (30%)
  • Wednesday: stakeholder meetings & 1:1s (20%)
  • Thursday: code reviews & mentoring (30%)
  • Friday: retrospective follow-ups & backlog refinement (20%)
Triage rules

When urgent production issues arise, I prioritize them and reallocate management time temporarily. I also maintain a deputy (senior engineer) who can make routine calls when I'm unavailable.
