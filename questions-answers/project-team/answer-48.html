Handling team disagreement with my approach

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: When the team disagrees with me, I encourage open debate, data-driven evaluation, and experiments. Final decisions balance technical soundness and delivery goals.

Example

The team disagreed with my plan to adopt GraphQL. I facilitated a spike with 2 team members, compared performance and developer experience to REST, and documented results. The team chose REST + JSON:API for now, leaving GraphQL on the roadmap.

Lessons
  • Empowering the team to test options builds ownership.
  • Documenting trade-offs prevents repeated debates.
