Identifying early risk to timeline and actions to take

System architect · Technical lead · Senior software engineer (Ruby on Rails, AWS, SQL, Vue.js)

Summary: Early risk is identified via deviations in key metrics (velocity drop, increased blockers), failing critical path tasks, and feedback from the team. I combine automated detection with proactive communication and corrective actions like re-scoping and resource reallocation.

Early warning signs
  • Missed daily progress on critical path stories.
  • Velocity trending down across sprints.
  • Rising count of blocked tickets or high review/PR time.
  • High uncertainty stories without spikes completed.
Immediate actions
  1. Assess impact: quantify how much of the timeline is at risk.
  2. Mitigate: run spikes, pair senior engineers on blockers, or bring in temporary help.
  3. Re-scope: cut non-essential scope to meet the committed date.
  4. Increase transparency: issue a risk report with current status and proposed mitigations.
Communication template for early risk
Subject: Risk: Feature X delivery at risk (current sprint)

Summary: 3 critical stories are blocked due to external API instability; estimated impact: 5 business days delay.

Actions taken:
- Started spike to mock API (owner: @alice)
- Paired senior engineer to unblock story (owner: @bob)
- Proposal: defer non-critical sub-feature (addresses) to next sprint

Request: approval to re-scope or allocate additional resources.
