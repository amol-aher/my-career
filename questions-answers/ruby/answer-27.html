Question 27: What about helpers and how are they different from concerns?

Concept

Helpers are view-layer modules (presentation logic). Concerns are for sharing behavior between models/controllers. They serve different layers of the MVC stack.

Optimal answer (concise)

Helpers live in app/helpers and are mixed into views/controllers for presentation. Concerns live in app/models/concerns or app/controllers/concerns and encapsulate shared domain or controller logic.

Subquestions & answers

  • Can helpers be used in models? — Avoid it; it couples model to view concerns.
  • When to use concerns? — When multiple models/controllers share behavior that is domain or controller-level.

Best practices / Pitfalls

  • Keep presentation logic in helpers; keep business logic in models/services.
