Question 45: What are Ruby’s basic data types?

Concept

Ruby's core data types include: NilClass, TrueClass, FalseClass, Integer, Float, String, Symbol, Array, Hash, Range, Regexp, Time, Proc, Object, and many more (classes are objects).

Optimal answer (concise)

Common primitive types: Integer, Float, String, Symbol, Array, Hash, booleans (true/false), and nil.

Subquestions & answers

  • Everything is an object? — Yes; classes, modules, and even integers are objects with methods.
  • Immediate values: Some objects (integers, symbols, true/false, nil) are immediate and not referenced by pointer in MRI.

Best practices / Pitfalls

  • Use appropriate types for clarity; avoid abusing symbols for dynamic keys in older Ruby versions.
