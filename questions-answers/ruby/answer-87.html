Question 87: What are Ruby’s key garbage collection algorithms?

Concept

Ruby uses generational garbage collection with mark-and-sweep. Over time MRI evolved from conservative mark-and-sweep to generational/incremental collectors, and later added compaction.

Optimal answer (concise)

Key algorithms: generational GC (young/old), incremental marking, and compaction to reduce fragmentation. JRuby uses JVM GCs (G1, Shenandoah, ZGC).

Subquestions & answers

  • Generational: frequent collection of young objects (most die quickly).
  • Incremental/Concurrent: reduce pause times via incremental marking and concurrent phases.

Best practices / Pitfalls

  • Monitor GC metrics; tune thresholds for workloads sensitive to pause times.
