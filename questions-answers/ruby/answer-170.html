Question 170: How would you choose between Fork + exec, Process.spawn, threads, and external services for parallelism in a production system?

Concept

This question is similar to earlier one (147) and emphasizes operational trade-offs: isolation, startup cost, shared memory, language/runtime, and observability.

Optimal answer (concise)

Choose fork+exec/spawn for running external programs or isolation; choose processes for CPU-bound tasks on MRI; choose threads/fibers for IO-bound concurrency and lower memory usage; choose external services for heavy compute or polyglot ecosystems. Consider startup latency, monitoring, restart behavior, and ease of deployment.

Checklist

  • Does task need full CPU parallelism? → processes or JRuby.
  • Need isolation/fault containment? → processes or external services.
  • Is low memory footprint critical? → threads/fibers.
  • Operational complexity acceptable? → external services/microservices.

Best practices

  • Prototype and measure; prefer simple designs and clear ownership boundaries.
