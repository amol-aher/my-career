Question 128: How do thread priorities work in Ruby? Are they reliable for scheduling?

Concept

Ruby exposes thread priorities via Thread#priority, but real scheduling depends on the OS and VM, and priority semantics are generally advisory.

Optimal answer (concise)

Thread priorities can hint at scheduling order but are not a reliable mechanism for correctness. Use explicit synchronization or work-stealing pools instead of depending on priority.

Subquestions & answers

  • Portability: behavior varies across platforms and Ruby implementations.
  • Alternatives: design explicit queues and scheduling rather than relying on priorities.

Best practices

  • Avoid depending on priorities for correctness; use them only for soft hints if necessary.
