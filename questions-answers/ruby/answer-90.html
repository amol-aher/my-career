Question 90: What profiling tools exist for Ruby?

Concept

Profiling tools include microbenchmarks and profilers for CPU and memory. Choose based on interpreter (MRI vs JRuby) and need (sampling vs instrumenting).

Optimal answer (concise)

Common tools: Benchmark/Benchmark.ips, ruby-prof, stackprof (sampling), rack-mini-profiler (web), memory_profiler, derailed_benchmarks, flamegraph tools, and async-profiler for JVM/JRuby.

Subquestions & answers

  • Sampling vs instrumenting: sampling profilers (stackprof) have lower overhead; instrumenting (ruby-prof) gives precise timings but higher overhead.
  • Flamegraphs: visualize stack traces over time to spot hot methods.

Example

# using stackprof
require 'stackprof'
StackProf.run(mode: :cpu, out: 'tmp/stackprof.dump', raw: true) do
  run_my_app_workload
end

Best practices / Pitfalls

  • Profile in an environment similar to production; warm up the VM and collect multiple samples.
