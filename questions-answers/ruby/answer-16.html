Question 16: What do you know about Ruby garbage collection?

Concept

Ruby's modern GC is generational and incremental: objects are allocated in the young generation; surviving objects are promoted; collection frequency and thresholds can be tuned.

Optimal answer (concise)

Ruby GC aims to balance throughput and latency via generations, incremental marking, and compaction improvements. Use GC.stat and tunables (RUBY_GC_*) for observability and tuning.

Subquestions & answers

  • What is promotion? — Objects surviving young-gen collections are promoted to older generation.
  • How to inspect GC? — GC.stat, ObjectSpace.each_object, and allocation tracers.

Best practices / Pitfalls

  • Monitor allocation churn and reduce temporary object creation.
  • Be cautious with finalizers and ObjectSpace operations — they add GC overhead.
