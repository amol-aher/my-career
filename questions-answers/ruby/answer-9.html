Question 9: Can you tell me something about Ruby’s journey (not Rails) — improvements from 1.8 to 3.0/3.2?

Concept

Ruby evolved significantly: 1.9 introduced YARV and encoding; 2.x refined performance and language features; 3.x focused on performance (3x goal), concurrency, and tooling (RBS/type support).

Optimal answer (concise)

Key milestones: YARV VM (1.9) for speed; encoding and fiber improvements; keyword argument changes; incremental GC and memory improvements; RBS/type tooling and fiber scheduler in Ruby 3.

Subquestions & answers

  • YARV: major speed-up over 1.8's interpreter.
  • Keyword args: behavior changed across 2.7→3.0 — positional vs keyword separation.
  • Tooling: RBS and TypeProf support static typing/analysis.
  • Performance: GC and method dispatch improvements, optional JIT experiments.

Best practices / Pitfalls

  • Test your gem compatibilities when upgrading between major Ruby versions.
  • Watch for keyword arg deprecation warnings migrating 2.7→3.0.
