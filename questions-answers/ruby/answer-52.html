Question 52: How do you declare constants in Ruby?

Concept

Constants in Ruby are variables with names beginning with an uppercase letter. They are intended not to change, though Ruby allows reassignment with warnings.

Optimal answer (concise)

Declare constants with uppercase names, e.g., PI = 3.14. They are lexically scoped within classes/modules.

Subquestions & answers

  • Can constants be changed? — Yes, but Ruby prints a warning.
  • Scope? — Constants obey lexical scoping and module/class namespaces.
  • Freeze? — Freeze objects assigned to constants for immutability guarantees.

Example

MAX_USERS = 100
module MathConst
  E = 2.718
end
