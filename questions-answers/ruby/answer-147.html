Question 147: How would you choose between Fork + exec, Process.spawn, threads, and external services for parallelism in a production system?

Concept

Choice depends on workload (CPU vs IO), isolation, resource usage, and deployment model.

Optimal answer (concise)

Fork+exec: good for replacing process image, isolation. Process.spawn: flexible child process creation. Threads: lightweight IO concurrency (limited by GVL on MRI). External services: best for scaling CPU-bound or language-diverse tasks.

Subquestions & answers

  • Isolation needs: processes/services isolate failures better.
  • Performance: threads cheaper for IO concurrency; processes better for CPU work on MRI.
  • Deployment: external services enable polyglot scaling (e.g., ML tasks in Python).

Best practices

  • Use threads for IO-bound concurrency, processes for CPU parallelism, and external services for specialized workloads.
