Question 10: In Ruby 3, how did they design internal interpreter, what architectural changes, what interpreter they are using, and what features or advantages were achieved?

Concept

Ruby MRI remains the canonical implementation using the YARV VM. Ruby 3 iterated on MRI internals (GC, method dispatch, fiber scheduler) rather than replacing the VM. Other implementations (JRuby, TruffleRuby) exist with different internals.

Optimal answer (concise)

Ruby 3 uses MRI/YARV with improvements: better GC, fiber scheduler for async IO, RBS support, and various performance patches — these improved throughput, reduced pause times, and enabled more ergonomic async integration.

Subquestions & answers

  • Is there a new VM? — No; MRI/YARV remains, but optimized.
  • Fiber scheduler? — New hooks let libraries implement non-blocking IO cooperatively.
  • Alternative interpreters? — JRuby (JVM), TruffleRuby (Graal-based) offer different performance characteristics.
