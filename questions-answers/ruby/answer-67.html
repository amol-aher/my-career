Question 67: How does garbage collection work in Ruby?

Concept

Modern Ruby uses generational garbage collection: newly allocated objects are in young generation and collected frequently; survivors are promoted. It uses mark-and-sweep and may compact memory to reduce fragmentation.

Optimal answer (concise)

Ruby GC marks reachable objects from roots, sweeps unmarked ones, and promotes surviving objects. Tunables and GC.stat provide observability and control.

Subquestions & answers

  • Generational advantage: most objects die young, reducing work.
  • Compaction: reduces fragmentation and improves locality.

Best practices / Pitfalls

  • Reduce allocation churn in hot paths; monitor GC pauses and tune RUBY_GC settings if needed.
